My teaching is grounded in participatory, dialogue-based pedagogy: I see learning as something built together, by connecting theory to lived experience and making room for every voice to contribute. I am pursuing this commitment formally through the University of Ottawa's Certificate in University Teaching.

---

##### Teaching Experience

**Faire dialoguer les mondes sociaux : Objets frontières & inclusion pédagogique** *(Bridging social worlds: Boundary objects & pedagogical inclusion)*
Micro-teaching session — November 20, 2025, University of Ottawa

A 20-minute micro-teaching session on boundary-object theory and its application to pedagogical inclusion, delivered as part of the Certificate in University Teaching. Learners moved through an active-learning sequence (BOPPPS model) — brainstorming individually, then synthesizing and sharing strategies in groups — to co-construct inclusion strategies grounded in the concept of boundary objects.

> "You are a welcoming, attentive instructor, well centred on your learners... [with an] excellent use of concrete examples, multiple avenues for engagement, and clear explanations of concepts."
> — Maryse Sullivan, Instructor, Certificate in University Teaching
